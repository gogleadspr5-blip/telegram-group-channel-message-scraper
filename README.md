🚀 How to Use the Telegram Message Scraper

Using the tool is designed to be straightforward and can be completed through the DataScrapify web platform.

Step 1 – Create Your DataScrapify Account

Create or log in to your DataScrapify account and access the Telegram scraping tools from the dashboard.

Step 2 – Connect Your Telegram Account

Follow the Telegram authentication process supported by DataScrapify. Depending on the workflow, Telegram API credentials may be required.

You can obtain your Telegram API credentials through Telegram's API development tools.

Step 3 – Select a Telegram Group or Channel

Choose the Telegram group or channel that you are authorized to access and want to analyze.

The available data depends on Telegram's access controls, account permissions, privacy settings, and community configuration.

Step 4 – Start Message Extraction

Start your scraping campaign from the DataScrapify dashboard. The platform processes the available Telegram conversation data and collects the supported information.

Step 5 – Review Your Results

Once processing is completed, review the collected information through the available results interface.

Depending on the accessible data, results can include messages, message IDs, usernames, Telegram user IDs, names, comments, and other available information.

Step 6 – Export Your Data

Download your results in CSV or Excel format.

The exported data can then be filtered, sorted, analyzed, used for reporting, or combined with other research datasets.
